# A single-input bound with balanced top values

For one general integer \(N\), the half-width class sampler and balanced top values give conditional expected bit time
\[
 L_N[1/3,\,1.9839824118071388229\ldots].
\]
A fixed rational 4,096-stage schedule at coefficient-width exponent \(\chi=1.01\) gives \(1.98458\). Two other fixed schedules at \(\chi=1.20\) and \(1.30\) give \(1.9946\) and \(1.9996\). The hypotheses are Dirichlet GRH and the analytic inputs of the pinned KeyBreak construction. Setup, private attempts, public searches, field-labelled extraction and restarts are charged to the current input. The analytic theorem is at manuscript level. ## Public field and top predicate

Keep the radix \(M=\lfloor N^{1/d}\rfloor\) of the degree parameter \(d\) and draw the field using the target \(kN\), for a fresh public prime \(k\in[M^C,2M^C]\) with sufficiently large fixed integer \(C\). The probability change from excluding divisors of \(N\) is negligible and can equally be treated as a successful proper-factor outcome. The ratios \(d'/d\to1\) and \((T_H/d'-T_H/d)/t\to0\) preserve every strict asymptotic budget and low cutoff margin. ## Interpreting the limiting bound

The finite schedule at width 1.01 has an exact integer certificate for its private screening charge. Lean computes the 4,096 rounded knots, proves the accumulator equals their normalized real sum, and checks that every stage costs strictly less than 1.984578735. The remaining finite charges are the rational comparisons in the cost table. [Finite gate proof](../formal/cogentic/Cogentic/RandomizedWidthOneGate.lean)

For the limit, enumerate every finite encoding accepted by the common schedule evaluator, including its rational parameters, pool sizes, confidence exponents and work caps. In phase $r$, start each index $j\le r$ with fresh randomness and give it $2^{r-j}$ charged steps. Allow a deterministic trial-division fallback $2^r$ steps as well. Stop on a verified proper divisor. Charge decoding, initialization and verification to the slots or to the common polynomial overhead. The fallback ensures termination on every tape. A fixed valid index on a sufficiently large composite has expected work $E_j$ to a verified split, including its internal restarts. From phase $r_*=j+\lceil\log_2(4\max(1,E_j))\rceil$ onwards, Markov gives conditional miss probability at most $1/4$ in each fresh slot. The expected sum of all charged phases is $O(2^j\max(1,E_j)\operatorname{poly}(\log N,j+\log(2+E_j)))$ since the survival probability decays by four while phase budgets double. This comparison holds for each fixed valid index regardless of the unknown input threshold at which its estimates apply. Taking the leading upper limit first and then the infimum over these indices yields the indicated coefficient. Primality tests and at most $O(\log N)$ nodes in the split tree preserve it.
