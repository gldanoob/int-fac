# Quartic norm construction and open checks

For the winning $p\equiv1\pmod3$ quadratic profile, the span of $-3$ and $-19$ also contains the squareclass $57$. Together these resolvents account for ten ternary dimensions and 16 active field bits. If their coordinates can be held fixed while the others retain the conditional prime distribution, the model reaches one expected collision pair at about 11.04 million samples. The corresponding binary ranks one, three and four give about 51.2 million, 2.54 million and 584,131 samples under the same assumption. The enumeration considers independent bases drawn from the active resolvents. [Exact enumeration and basis coverage](../norm-bases.txt)

The fixture works in $K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3},\sqrt{-19})$. Using a certified 1,315-bit seed prime $s$, a valuation condition above $s$ and a ray congruence, it constructs the integral primitive quartic $P(j)=\operatorname{Nm}_{K/\mathbb Q}(\alpha+j\beta)/s^3$. The builder checks the lifts, lattice congruences, content one and irreducibility over $\mathbb Q$. Its coefficients have 2,671 to 2,673 bits. From this directory, run `python3 build.py` with `gp` on the path to regenerate the JSON. [Builder](build.py) · [Exact lattice, coefficients and field order](construction.json)

The remaining obligations are to verify the predicted cubic Artin classes for prime values of $P$, measure or bound variation in the other active fields, and exhibit and certify an actual collision. No prime-value scan or height-six certificate accompanies this quartic.

## Possible extension to larger heights

A proposed way to reconstruct dependencies among cubic fields sharing a resolvent uses their Cardano radicals. For $x^3+a x^2+b x+c$, put $P=a^2-3b$, $Q=2a^3-9ab+27c$ and $u=(-Q\pm\sqrt{Q^2-4P^3})/2$, choosing a nonzero radical. Its norm in a nontrivial quadratic mirror is $P^3$. The mirror field is $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3\Delta})$ for polynomial discriminant $\Delta$. Products of cubic characters at split test primes could detect independent radical classes. To certify a proposed dependence exactly, for a nonrational product $z$ with norm $r^3$, test rational roots $t$ of $t^3-3rt=\operatorname{Tr}(z)$ and check $((z+tr)/(t^2-r))^3=z$ when the denominator is nonzero. Rational products and degenerate mirrors need separate treatment. This is an unimplemented proposal. The explicit field inclusions in the [height-six proof](../proof.md) give the certificates used for this handoff.
