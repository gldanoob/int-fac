# Quartic norm construction and open checks

For the winning $p\equiv1\pmod3$ quadratic profile, the span of $-3$ and $-19$ also contains the squareclass $57$. Together these resolvents account for ten ternary dimensions and 16 active field bits. If their coordinates can be held fixed while the others retain the conditional prime distribution, the model reaches one expected collision pair at about 11.04 million samples. The corresponding binary ranks one, three and four give about 51.2 million, 2.54 million and 584,131 samples under the same assumption. The enumeration considers independent bases drawn from the active resolvents. [Exact enumeration and basis coverage](../norm-bases.txt)

The fixture works in $K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3},\sqrt{-19})$. Using a certified 1,315-bit seed prime $s$, a valuation condition above $s$ and a ray congruence, it constructs the integral primitive quartic $P(j)=\operatorname{Nm}_{K/\mathbb Q}(\alpha+j\beta)/s^3$. The builder checks the lifts, lattice congruences, content one and irreducibility over $\mathbb Q$. Its coefficients have 2,671 to 2,673 bits. From this directory, run `python3 build.py` with `gp` on the path to regenerate the JSON. [Builder](build.py) · [Exact lattice, coefficients and field order](construction.json)

## Splitting at prime values

For every prime $t=P(j)$ coprime to the seed, the conductors and the field discriminants, **all sixteen cubics with resolvent discriminant $-3,-19$ or $57$ split completely**. The remaining 54 active irreducibility bits can be measured separately.

Write $w=\alpha+j\beta$, $C=29\,099\,070$, and let $I$ be the prime of $K$ defined by the two chosen roots above $s$. Its residue degree is one. The two simple Hensel lifts to modulus $s^3$ define the map $\mathcal O_K\to\mathbb Z/s^3$ at $I$, with kernel $I^3$. Indeed the basis $1,u,v,uv$ is integral and spans the full ring of integers since the two quadratic discriminants are coprime. The completed split factor at $I$ is $\mathbb Z_s$. The construction gives $w\in I^3$ and $w\equiv1\pmod {C\mathcal O_K}$. Consequently $(w)=I^3 T$ for an integral ideal $T$ of absolute norm $t$. As $K/\mathbb Q$ is Galois and $t$ is unramified, a prime ideal with norm $t$ implies that $t$ splits completely in $K$.

For any of the three quadratic subfields $E$, take ideal norms to $E$. Both $I$ and $T$ contract with relative residue degree one. With $I_E$ and $T_E$ their contractions, $(\mathrm{Nm}_{K/E}w)=I_E^3 T_E$. Its generator is congruent to one modulo $C$. At the two real places of $E=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{57})$ the norm is positive. The cubic extension in a represented $S_3$ closure over $E$ has conductor $f\mathcal O_E$, with field discriminant $D_E f^2$. For the archived sixteen fields, every $f$ divides $C$. Its Artin map therefore kills the principal ideal on the left and the cube $I_E^3$ on the right. It kills $T_E$, giving complete splitting in that cubic closure. [Cohen–Thorne, equation (1.3) and the conductor formula](https://thornef.github.io/countDLgen-final.pdf) · [Milne, Artin reciprocity for ray classes](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/CFT.pdf)

The construction also fixes the remaining public characters. Modulo $C$, $s^3t\equiv1$, so $t$ is a cube at each cyclic conductor. Modulo the marker modulus $M$ the element $w$ lies in the image of $\mathcal O_{\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})}$ because its last two coordinates vanish. Hence $s^3t=\mathrm{Nm}(w)$ is a rational square modulo $M$. At each odd marker prime the Legendre symbol therefore agrees with the seed's. The unit square modulo eight is one and gives the seed's characters at $-1$ and $2$. Together with $s\equiv1\bmod24$ this gives the designated quadratic profile on prime values coprime to $M$.

The replay recomputes the quartic norm by nested resultants, checks all marker signs, proves primality and factors each of the 369 defining cubics over each residue field. The three prime values at $j=532,683,1238$ have 2,707, 2,708 and 2,712 bits. All sixteen prescribed cubics split at each. Of the 54 remaining active fields, respectively 38, 38 and 35 were irreducible. The distribution of these 54 bits along the univariate sequence and a height-six miss remain open. ## Possible extension to larger heights

A proposed way to reconstruct dependencies among cubic fields sharing a resolvent uses their Cardano radicals. For $x^3+a x^2+b x+c$, put $P=a^2-3b$, $Q=2a^3-9ab+27c$ and $u=(-Q\pm\sqrt{Q^2-4P^3})/2$, choosing a nonzero radical. Its norm in a nontrivial quadratic mirror is $P^3$. The mirror field is $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3\Delta})$ for polynomial discriminant $\Delta$. Products of cubic characters at split test primes could detect independent radical classes. To certify a proposed dependence exactly, for a nonrational product $z$ with norm $r^3$, test rational roots $t$ of $t^3-3rt=\operatorname{Tr}(z)$ and check $((z+tr)/(t^2-r))^3=z$ when the denominator is nonzero. Rational products and degenerate mirrors need separate treatment. This is an unimplemented proposal. The explicit field inclusions in the [height-six proof](../proof.md) give the certificates used for this handoff.
