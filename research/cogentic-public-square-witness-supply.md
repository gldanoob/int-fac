# Public square witnesses supply prescribed valuation rows

For every odd \(N>1\), public witnesses can fill any supplied finite set of rational valuation-parity rows and the sign row. The witnesses are constructed deterministically and verified modulo \(N\). This gives projected square-class completion, while exact matching also requires parity at the primes outside the supplied set. A density bound below shows that public witnesses with short numerical multipliers and small residuals generically add private prime rows outside a bounded sheared mark span. [Exact rational square matching](cogentic-rational-root-matching.md) · [Sheared evaluation labels](cogentic-sheared-local-squares.md)

## Construction for a prescribed set of rows

Let \(S\) be a finite set of distinct primes coprime to \(N\), and put \(Q=\prod_{q\in S}q^2\). A prime sharing a proper gcd with \(N\) already gives a divisor. For each \(p\in S\), use CRT to choose \(k_p\) in \((-Q,0]\) with
\[
 1-k_pN\equiv p\pmod{p^2},\qquad
 1-k_pN\equiv1\pmod{q^2}\quad(q\in S,\ q\ne p).
\]
The moduli are coprime and the coefficient \(N\) is invertible modulo each. The positive integer \(E_p=1-k_pN\) has valuation one at \(p\) and zero at the other selected primes. Add the negative label \(E_-=1-QN\), which is one modulo every \(q^2\). All labels are units modulo \(N\) with the public root \(t=1\). This also works for \(p=2\).

On the sign and selected valuation rows the resulting matrix is the identity, so the full rational square classes of these generators are independent as well. A requested projection is realized by multiplying the corresponding labels and still has root one. The product can again be written \(1-kN\) since it reduces to one. Label lengths are polynomial in \(\log N\) and \(\sum_{q\in S}\log q\), even when writing the products. CRT, gcd tests and label checks take polynomial bit work in the explicit prime-list length and \(\log N\). Thus a list of \(\exp(O(W))\) primes of polynomial bit length has one-third cost, where \(W=(\log N)^{1/3}(\log\log N)^{2/3}\). The numerical multiplier can be as large as \(Q\). Taking only the selected parity rows would discard the possible odd valuations at other primes, and therefore cannot certify exact matching.

## Private primes for short multipliers and small residuals

Fix \(c,C>0\) and a dyadic interval \(X\le N<2X\), and evaluate \(W\) at \(X\). Use \(M=\lfloor\sqrt N\rfloor\), \(A=\lfloor e^{cW}\rfloor\) and the entire sheared mark family
\[
 s=a-b(M-j),\qquad |a|,b\le A,\quad b>0,\quad |j|\le e^{CW},\quad \gcd(a,b)=1.
\]
Include the rational sign and allow all products and quotients of these labels. For the public witnesses allow *every* integer pair \((t,k)\) subject to \(\gcd(t,N)=1\), \(|k|\le e^{CW}\), and \(0<|t^2-kN|\le X^{1/2} e^{CW}\). Then outside at most \(X\exp(-\Omega_{c,C}(W))\) inputs, the following holds simultaneously. Group the positive-multiplier public witnesses by equality of \(t^2/k\). One class from each group is independent modulo the rational span of *all* the sheared marks and the sign. The conclusion in particular holds for choices made after inspecting \(N\).

To prove this, freeze \(M\) and vary \(r=N-M^2\) in \(0\le r\le2M\). The mark values are fixed nonzero integers in this block. Representatives equal to \(\pm1\) have no prime support. Pure rational primitive labels add only a sign. A qualifying public witness has \(t\ne0\) by the unit condition. For sufficiently large \(X\), \(k<0\) gives a residual at least \(N\), outside the height cap. The case \(k=0\) is an ordinary rational square with the corresponding global-sign root. For each remaining \(k>0\), a qualifying \(|t|\) lies within \(e^{O_C(W)}\) of \(\sqrt{k}M\), since \(|t^2-kM^2|\le X^{1/2}e^{CW}+2kM\). There are \(e^{O_C(W)}\) resulting affine forms \(t^2-k(M^2+r)\) per block. All their values throughout the block have absolute value at most \(M e^{O_C(W)}\), and each form is injective in \(r\).

Choose \(y=e^{D W}\) for a sufficiently large fixed \(D=D(c,C)\). The [Euler product count for even valuations above \(y\)](cogentic-centered-quadratic-density.md#a-count-of-possible-norms), applied with \(\lambda=\log\log M/(4\log y)\), gives at most \(M\exp(-\Omega_D(V))\) indices per form with zero residual or all prime valuations above \(y\) even. Here \(V=(\log X)^{2/3}(\log\log X)^{1/3}\), and the \(e^{O_C(W)}\) norm inflation and the entire menu size are negligible on this scale. Thus almost all inputs have an odd valuation above \(y\) in every qualifying public form.

If a rational prime \(p>y\) divides two public forms in different groups, it divides \(k't^2-kt'^2\ne0\). If it divides a public form and a mark label \(s\), it belongs to the fixed prime support of that \(s\). These nonzero integers have \(O_C(\log X)\) bits and hence at most that many prime divisors. For each candidate \(p>y\), the public form can vanish at only one class of \(r\bmod p\), since \(p>k\). This costs at most \(O(1/p+1/M)\) of the block. There are \(e^{O_{c,C}(W)}\) pairs of forms and pairs of a form and a mark. Taking \(D\) larger than their count exponent makes this total loss \(\exp(-\Omega_{c,C}(W))\). Summing over the blocks gives the claimed exception bound. Hence every public group has an odd prime row shared with neither another group nor any mark product. The same density statement holds inside any fixed balanced semiprime population of size \(\gg X/\log^2 X\).

Within a group, the labels satisfy \(E'/E=k'/k=(t'/t)^2>0\), so equality of slope ratios includes the rational square factor and preserves the sign of the label. The roots differ by the same rational factor up to global sign. This factor is a unit modulo \(N\) because both \(t,t'\) are units. A rational matching dependency must use each group with even total multiplicity by its private row. For a representative \((E,t)\), a group subtotal of \(2j\) copies has normalized root \(t^{2j}/|E|^j\equiv\operatorname{sgn}(E)^j\pmod N\), a global sign. The same identity holds for negative multiplicities. Thus adjoining this entire public menu changes neither which local square classes can match nor which productive signs they supply on the stated inputs. This bound covers the *numerical* multiplier and residual envelopes above. Public witnesses with larger multipliers or residuals, including the general prescribed-row construction, fall outside the bound.
