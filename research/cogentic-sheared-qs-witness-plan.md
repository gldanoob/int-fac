# Secondary plan for near-root public witnesses

Use the nine integers in `experiments/cogentic-sheared-scaling-inputs.json` in published order. This is a secondary experiment on an existing cohort with known field-only baseline outcomes. Keep height 64, bases $\lfloor\sqrt N\rfloor-j$ for $j=0,1,2$, slopes $0,-1$ in each base and the same exact signed quadratic local kernel as the published scaling study. The bounded rational mark prime cutoff stays 16,384. No changes to the published results are made.

Attach exactly the public candidates $r=\lceil\sqrt N\rceil+j$ for $j=-32,-31,\ldots,32$ in increasing order, labelled by $E=r^2-N$. Skip zero or nonunit labels or nonpositive roots. A nonunit derivative causes the corresponding field to be skipped. Neither type of gcd counts as a split. The search receives only $N$, never the cohort labels or factors.

Factor only the 65 auxiliary integers $|E|$ by bounded trial division through their square roots. On these inputs $|E|<2^{39}$ and the prime sieve need never exceed $2^{20}$. Extract every prime above 16,384 in the union of the auxiliary supports from each base mark value and each auxiliary label, giving separate exact rows for those primes. Use the original exact bounded mark squareclass procedure on the reduced labels. The large residuals from field marks were already disjoint or equal, so extraction preserves that property. The reduced public labels are smooth through 16,384. Compute field-only, public-only and full rational kernels in original field-basis order followed by increasing public $j$ order.

Test a basis of the sum of the field-only and public-only kernels first, in that order, then independent extensions from the full kernel in elimination order. Share a cap of 32 completed exact root tuples across all three groups. Recover exact local roots only when testing a chosen tuple. Compare the complete sign images of the two restricted domains and the full domain wherever the cap permits, reporting rank lower bounds for any incompletely tested domain. Attribute a new mixed improvement only when the full sign-image rank exceeds the rank of the sum of the two restricted images, with both restricted images certified complete. Do not stop at a first successful gcd. Report any partial prefix under a cap. Use 15 seconds wall time per input including preparation, with no retries or cap increases.

After all searches, regenerate the cohort factor labels and independently verify any emitted field roots and normalized modular certificates. Public-only and field-only factors are explicitly separated from new mixed splits.
